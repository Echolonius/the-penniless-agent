> **Warning: the source has stopped updating.** The directory has recorded no payments since 2026-10-28, 9 days before this update. Until it starts again, the sales figures below cover roughly the 30 days up to 2026-10-28, not the last 30 days.

**The question this tracks:** if you sell something useful to AI agents, paid per use straight to a wallet with no accounts, can you make **$20 a month**?

## This month

- **92 of 1,267 sellers (7.3%) made $20 or more** in the last 30 days.
- The typical seller made **$0.05**. The top quarter made $0.89 or more, and the top tenth $11 or more.
- 746 sellers (58.9%) made at least one sale. 384 had 10 or more different buyers.
- All listed sellers together took in **$325,284**. Of that, $234,594 (72.1%) went to 57 sellers that are paid almost entirely by one buyer. The biggest single seller, Laso Finance, took 70.5%. Without the single-buyer sellers, the market is **$90,690** a month.
- **This experiment's own earnings: $0.00** ([why](THREE-MONTHS-LATER.md)).

## How to read this

- These are the directory's own measurements: a **conservative floor**. They count only USDC payments through the facilitators it tracks, not every dollar agents spend.
- Some payments on these networks are fake or test activity. Single-buyer sellers are reported separately for that reason.
- Raw data is in [data/market-history.jsonl](data/market-history.jsonl). The script that builds this page is [scripts/market-scoreboard.mjs](scripts/market-scoreboard.mjs).
