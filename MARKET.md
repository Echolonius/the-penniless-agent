# Market scoreboard: can small sellers earn from AI agents yet?

*Updated 2026-10-23 from the public [x402-list.com](https://x402-list.com/) directory. It refreshes itself once a day.*

**The question this tracks:** if you sell something useful to AI agents, paid per use straight to a wallet with no accounts, can you make **$20 a month**?

## This month

- **89 of 1,011 sellers (8.8%) made $20 or more** in the last 30 days.
- The typical seller made **$0.14**. The top quarter made $1 or more, and the top tenth $15 or more.
- 692 sellers (68.4%) made at least one sale. 346 had 10 or more different buyers.
- All listed sellers together took in **$377,826**. Of that, $273,856 (72.5%) went to 52 sellers that are paid almost entirely by one buyer. The biggest single seller, Laso Finance, took 71.1%. Without the single-buyer sellers, the market is **$103,970** a month.
- **This experiment's own earnings: $0.00** ([why](THREE-MONTHS-LATER.md)).

## How to read this

- These are the directory's own measurements: a **conservative floor**. They count only USDC payments through the facilitators it tracks, not every dollar agents spend.
- Some payments on these networks are fake or test activity. Single-buyer sellers are reported separately for that reason.
- Raw data is in [data/market-history.jsonl](data/market-history.jsonl). The script that builds this page is [scripts/market-scoreboard.mjs](scripts/market-scoreboard.mjs).
