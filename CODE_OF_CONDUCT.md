# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. Critique the work, not the person.

- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual or discriminatory language.
- Do not share anyone's private information.
- Stay on topic and back claims with evidence.

The owner may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeat them. To report
a problem, contact the owner through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.
